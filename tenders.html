Public sector buyers across the UK publish contracts on tender portals. Winning one starts with being registered, finding the right notices, and sending a complete, well written bid. Our Tender Specialist, Rita Omotegho Aghwana, helps small businesses with each step.

Who this is for

This is for small businesses that want to win UK public sector work and are new to tendering, short of time, or unsure where to start. We mainly work with couriers and logistics firms, construction and trades, care and healthcare services, cleaning and facilities companies, and other small service businesses, whether you're a sole trader just starting out or a limited company ready to grow into public contracts.

How we help

Portal registration

We help you set up and complete your registration on the UK tender portals you need, so you can see and respond to opportunities.

Finding contracts that fit

We screen notices against what your business does, where it works, and how much it can realistically deliver.

Preparing documents

We help you gather, organise, and check the company information and supporting documents a buyer asks for.

Bid writing

We write clear, complete responses that answer each question and speak to how the bid will be scored.

What we need from you

  • Your company details and a description of the services you offer
  • The areas where you can work and the size of contract you can handle
  • Supporting documents a buyer may ask for, such as policies, insurances, and accreditations
  • Examples of similar work you have done, if you have them
  • The tender notice or link and the closing date, if you have a specific tender in mind

Timing

Timelines depend on the portal, the tender's requirements and closing date, and how much information your business has ready. We confirm the scope and schedule with you before you commit.

If a closing date is close, say so in your enquiry. Time-sensitive requests are welcome, and we will tell you what we can support.

Common questions

Which portals do you work with?

We work across the main UK public sector portals, so you don't have to work out which one a contract sits on. That covers Find a Tender and Contracts Finder for England and UK-wide opportunities, Sell2Wales for Wales, Public Contracts Scotland for Scotland, and eTendersNI for Northern Ireland. Many NHS trusts, councils and housing associations also run their own supplier portals, and we handle registration on those too. We'll register you where it matters, set up alerts for your type of work, and flag the contracts that actually suit your size.

Can you help if my business has never bid for public sector work?

Yes. Many of the businesses we help are new to tendering. We start with registration and finding contracts that suit you, then move on to documents and bids.

Do you guarantee we will win a contract?

No. Nobody can promise an award. Our focus is a complete, clear, well prepared bid that gives you the best chance of being scored well.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need, whether that's portal registration, finding suitable contracts, preparing your documents or writing a full bid. We'll give you a fixed price in writing before any work starts, so you know exactly what you're paying. Get in touch and tell us what you're after, and we'll send you a quote.

What do you need to get started?

A description of your business, the areas you cover, and any tender you are already considering, with its closing date.
