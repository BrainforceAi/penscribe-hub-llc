Frequently asked questions

Government contracting, without the guesswork.

01

What does a government contracting consultant do?

We help businesses assess opportunities, satisfy registrations and certifications, build capture strategy, and prepare compliant proposals for federal, state, and local agencies.

02

Can The Penscribe write my entire government proposal?

Yes. Full-service support can include the compliance matrix, technical and management volumes, past performance, pricing narrative, forms, reviews, and submission support.

03

How do I know if an RFP is worth bidding?

Our go/no-go review examines eligibility, capability fit, past performance, competition, evaluation criteria, schedule, and pricing feasibility before you invest.

04

Do you work with first-time government contractors?

Yes. We help qualified service businesses establish the registrations, positioning, proof, and pursuit process required to compete credibly.
