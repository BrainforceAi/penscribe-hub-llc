    Common questions

    What is a capability statement?

    It is a short document that tells buyers who you are, what you do, which codes and certifications you hold, and what work you have done. Contracting officers and prime contractors use it to decide whether to talk to you.

    Does it have to be one page?

    One page is the standard because it is read quickly. I design yours as a one-page PDF.

    Can you update the one I already have?

    Yes. Send me your current version and I will review it and tell you what to change.

    Do you do the design as well as the writing?

    Yes. I handle the writing and positioning and deliver a designed one-page PDF.

    What do you need from me?

    A company overview, your target buyers, your codes and certifications, and your past performance. A logo and your current statement help if you have them.

    Is there a charge to talk first?

    If our first conversation is enough, there is no charge.
