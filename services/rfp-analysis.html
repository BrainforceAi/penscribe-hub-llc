RFP and Solicitation Analysis

Detailed breakdown of RFPs and RFQs: risks, requirements, evaluation criteria, and a go or no-go recommendation.

Before you spend money on a proposal, you need to know whether the contract is winnable. We read the full solicitation, identify scoring weights, flag compliance risks, evaluate fit against your capabilities, and give you a clear go or no-go in writing.

Deliverables

  • Section-by-section solicitation summary
  • Evaluation criteria and scoring weight breakdown
  • Compliance risk register
  • Capability gap analysis
  • Competitor positioning notes
  • Written go or no-go recommendation

Timeline

Turnaround is 48 hours for most solicitations. The first review is free if you are a new client.

How to start

Send us a short note describing your business and your situation. If there is a specific solicitation on the table, include it. We will respond within one business day with next steps. If the first conversation is enough, there is no charge.
