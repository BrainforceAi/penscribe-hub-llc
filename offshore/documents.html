Submitting your documents

How we collect documents, step by step

We ask only for what each stage needs, so your most sensitive details are shared as late as possible.

1. Application

CV, passport details, qualifications, relevant certificates and employment history.

2. Screening

The employer's HR reviews your application and may interview you or verify your documents.

3. Job offer

Once you are selected and receive an offer, HR may ask for payroll information.

4. Onboarding

You send bank details directly to the employer's authorised HR or payroll team.

Bank details: Bank account details may be required during final employment onboarding for payroll. At the application stage we focus on your professional and employment documents. We never ask for bank details, card numbers or passwords when you apply. If anyone does, read our scam warning.
