Frequently asked questions

Do you charge fees to applicants?

No. We never charge for a job placement. Only optional services (CV and certification) are paid.

How do applications work?

You complete one form per job. We review it and submit your application to the employer for you.

Why did a job disappear?

Vacancies are removed automatically when they close or are filled.
