Questions about CV services

Do I have to buy a package to apply for jobs?

No. Applying for jobs on our site is always free. CV services are optional.

Can you guarantee I will get a job?

No one honestly can. We make you a stronger, better-prepared candidate. Employers make the hiring decision.

I have no offshore experience. Which package?

Standard or Premium. We present your transferable skills and certificates, and plan the right entry-level roles.

Do you need my passport or ID?

Only for the certificate check and application pack, and only when we ask. Don't send ID documents by chat.

How do I pay?

After your order we email an invoice and payment instructions. Pay only to details sent from our official email address.

Can I add other documents later?

Yes. See our document services for cover letters, translations, checks and more.
