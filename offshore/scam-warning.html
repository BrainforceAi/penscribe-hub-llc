Scam warning

Fake offshore recruiters are common. Protect yourself.

How to spot a fake job offer

  • We never charge for a job, interview, visa or flight.
  • Be cautious of offers from free email accounts or unknown WhatsApp numbers.
  • Be wary of unrealistic salaries or instant offers with no interview.
  • Never pay "medical", "processing" or "ticket" fees to a person.
  • Never send bank details, card numbers or passwords when you apply. Genuine employers ask for payroll details only after a job offer, during onboarding, and direct to their own HR or payroll team.
  • Optional paid services (CV, certification) are listed only on this website.
